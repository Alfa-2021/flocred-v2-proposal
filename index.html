AI paperwork

Paperwork done in seconds, not hours

Claude AI, already part of FLOCRED’s technology, reads and prepares the paperwork for every loan file, so the team spends its time with customers instead of typing.

Reads every document

PAN, Aadhaar, bank statements, salary slips, ITR, GST and property papers, photographed in the field and read by AI in seconds.

Checks before anything goes out

Names, dates and numbers are matched across documents. Missing, expired or blurry papers are flagged, and the borrower gets a WhatsApp reminder.

Analyses bank statements

Monthly income, average balance, bounces and existing EMIs, summarised for the eligibility check.

Reads CIBIL reports

Score, active loans, overdue history and recent enquiries on one screen, feeding straight into lender matching.

Fills lender forms

Each bank and NBFC’s application format is filled from the file, ready for Ops to review and submit. No retyping.

Writes the office paperwork

DSA agreements, offer letters, payout statements, sanction-letter summaries for customers and the daily MIS, drafted automatically.

A person approves every AI result before anything goes to a lender or customer. Files stay in FLOCRED’s India-hosted storage, Aadhaar numbers are masked, and only the pages needed are sent for reading.
Digital marketing

Google and Meta ads that bring loans, not just leads

Leads from Facebook, Instagram and Google flow straight into the CRM, and every disbursal is reported back to the ad platforms, so the budget goes where real borrowers come from.

Meta lead ads, synced live

Facebook and Instagram instant-form leads reach the CRM within seconds, are assigned by zone and get a WhatsApp welcome message.

Google Ads leads, tagged

Lead-form and website leads carry the campaign, ad and keyword, so you see exactly which searches bring business.

Results sent back to the ads

Sanctioned and disbursed files go back to Meta’s Conversions API and Google’s offline conversions (phone numbers hashed first), so the ads find more real borrowers.

Cost per disbursal, not just per lead

Spend, cost per lead and cost per disbursal by campaign, city and loan type, on one dashboard.

Optional add-on

AI calling: every lead called within a minute

ARIA Voice calls each new lead in Hindi or English, asks the basic questions, books a visit with the right executive and saves a call summary in the CRM. It can also chase missing documents and callbacks.

  • Introduces itself as FLOCRED’s AI assistant and hands over to a person whenever asked
  • Calls only people who asked to be contacted, following TRAI and DND rules
  • Calls are recorded with consent, and the summary is saved to the lead
  • Switch it on or off any time, and pay only for the minutes used
Alfa Team Management also plans and runs Google and Meta campaigns. Ads management is an optional monthly service, separate from the platform build.

How this is calculated
  • Each field executive saves the minutes you choose per day on reports, DSR updates and attendance, over 26 working days.
  • Each team lead saves 45 minutes a day on status calls and manual reports.
  • Each lead saves 6 minutes of forwarding, duplicate checks and status updates.
  • Each loan file saves about 25 minutes of reading documents, checking them and filling lender forms, thanks to AI paperwork.
  • One full-time person works about 208 hours a month. One field visit takes about 40 minutes.
  • These are planning estimates to discuss together, not guaranteed results.
